# Design direction and review

## Public page

Dealt with the taste skill: **Colour chapters**, seed 1442443008. It replaces the earlier quiet-instrument page, which told the older clash-desk story.

**Idea:** the hours of one storm-hit Sunday become chapters of flat colour, with the real timetable as the live control.

- **Type:** Albert Sans, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.
- **Palette:** grey, mint and orange, with black and white for text.
- **Live control:** pressing a finish time and a shortest set rearranges the real Bonnaroo timetable and says what it cost. "Show what really happened" compares it with the day as reconstructed.

## Studio

The working desk keeps its **framed-sheet** app style (seed 444071102), now in Albert Sans: a tinted frame, a white sheet, and colour on data only. The timetable needs the full width, so the style's sidebar stays dropped.

## Review

**Final inspector results, 30 October:** public page 0 FAIL, 1 WARN at 1440, 768 and 390; studio 0 FAIL, 0 WARN. The one warning is the count of font sizes (15), because the fluid steps and the live timetable add sizes beyond the five steps. Both sets of screenshots were read by eye.
