How a day is repaired

First, the bill is read against Qloo.

Each act ranks every other act on the bill. Pairs that rank each other near the top and play at once are clashes.

Then a message from production arrives.

Gemma proposes constraints, each quoted word for word. Nothing changes until you tick it.

Last, you approve one repair.

The solver keeps every rule, and shows what the same repair would look like without Qloo.
