Before the announcement

Does a cue mean shared fans?

It means aggregate cultural association. Counterset keeps those cues separate from actual attendee selections and unknown relationships. It does not forecast attendance.

Can I keep an intentional clash?

Yes. Declare intentional pairs in the production facts or lock an act to its current placement. The solver respects those choices.

What if the day is impossible?

The studio reports infeasibility and a conflicting constraint set. It preserves durations, locks and curfews while you decide what can change.

What happens to the model’s notes?

Gemma or Flash Lite extracts a proposed constraint with its original quote. Nothing changes until the programmer approves it.
